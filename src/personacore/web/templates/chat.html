{# chat.html — the conversation. The front door of the product. CHANGED FROM THE APPROVED DESIGN, twice and deliberately. The design drew "one message, one reply — a diagnostic, not a conversation" and replaced the exchange region on every send. The owner asked for a conversation after seeing it, and then for the rest of what a chat product is. The design's exchange is kept exactly as drawn and is still the unit that repeats; what changed is everything around it. LAYOUT: three regions and no page scroll. The rail of earlier conversations on the left, the messages in the middle scrolling on their own, the composer pinned under them. The messages scroll, the page does not — which is what keeps the box you type in and the message you are reading next to each other however long the conversation gets. SWAP BOUNDARIES, all three of them, because feeling fast is a set of decisions about what is redrawn: * SEND posts to /admin/chat/fragment and APPENDS one exchange to #chat-messages (hx-swap="beforeend"). Nothing else on the page is touched, so the scroll position, the composer and the picker survive a reply. chat.js draws the question immediately and removes its own placeholder when the real one lands. * SWITCHING gets /admin/chat/messages and replaces #chat-messages alone, pushing /admin/chat?c=… so the address still names the thread. Without JavaScript the same control is the same link and loads the same page. * The PERSONA form is an ordinary post that re-renders the page. It changes who answers in THIS conversation and nothing outside it — the core's default persona, and every other surface using it, are left alone — but it decides the voice and the name under every future reply here, so it should look like something happened. Same for the NAME AND GROUP form in the header: renaming moves the rail row and the heading over the messages, and redrawing one field would hide most of that. NO SCRIPT AT ALL: the composer declares method="post" action="/admin/chat", so the browser posts it and the server redirects back to the conversation with the reply in it.
