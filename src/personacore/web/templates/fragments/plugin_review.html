Until this dialog, the declaration was visible only to somebody who unzipped the archive and opened a TOML file. That is not a disclosure. THE WORDING IS THE FEATURE, AND IT IS NOT WRITTEN HERE. Every load-bearing sentence — the lede, the two "Enforced." notes, the "Not enforced." note and the closing paragraph — is a named constant in ui/routes.py, passed in.
