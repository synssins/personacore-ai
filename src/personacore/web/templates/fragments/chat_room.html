Both are edited HERE, over the messages, in the design's ordinary field-and-button controls — not in a dialog and not in a second screen, because renaming a thread is something you do while looking at it. ONE FORM FOR BOTH, and one button. Naming a conversation and filing it are the same errand — somebody tidying does both in the same breath — and two buttons side by side would ask which of the two they meant. The group field is free text with the groups already in use offered as suggestions (a , so typing a new one is never blocked). There is no table of groups on purpose: a group is a label, and a label with a lifecycle of its own is a second thing to keep in step for no benefit. It carries its own id because it is swapped out of band when another thread is opened without a page load: the heading over the messages has to be the heading of the messages underneath it. `manages_rooms` false is a store that knows about conversations but not about naming them. The controls are then ABSENT rather than disabled — a button that silently does nothing is worse than one that is not there — and the sentence says which. #}
